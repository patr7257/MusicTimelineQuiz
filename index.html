♪ Music Timeline Quiz

Hear a song, then drop it into your timeline by the year it came out. Right spot, you keep the card. First to the target wins.

Each player scans the on-screen QR with their phone camera to play the song on Spotify. The link goes straight to the track, so whoever scans should just press play, not read the title. The screen keeps the year and category hidden until you reveal.
